three steps

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in on the platform’s own page

    Instagram or Google show their usual sign-in page; in Chrome, the sign-in you already have in the browser is enough. Your password is typed only there — Shuffler never sees it.

  2. 2

    We find your old likes

    Shuffler gathers the list of Reels and Shorts you liked — right on your device.

  3. 3

    Swipe — no repeats

    Random order, and nothing repeats until the round is over. Then a new round starts, in a different order.

privacy

Everything stays with you

Shuffler isn’t a social network or a service with an account. It’s a calm way to rewatch what you already liked.

Privacy Policy
  • Your library stays on your deviceYour likes, watch history and settings are stored with you, not with us.
  • No servers of oursShuffler doesn’t send your data to us — we have nowhere to keep it.
  • No ads, no trackingNo banners, no analytics, no third-party pixels — in the app or on this site.
  • Every video links to its originalShuffler downloads nothing: videos stream from the platform where they were posted and open there in one tap.

Shuffler is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Meta, Instagram, Google or YouTube. Platform names are mentioned only to explain where your likes come from; they are trademarks of their owners.

Is it safe?

You sign in on the official Instagram or Google page, so Shuffler never sees or stores your password. The app reads your list of likes and doesn’t post or change anything in your account unless you explicitly ask it to.

What do you store?

Nothing on our side — Shuffler has no servers. Your library of likes, what you’ve watched and your settings live only on your device and are removed together with the app or extension.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Why do I need to sign in?

Your likes are visible only to you. To find them, Shuffler opens them on your behalf — the same way the platform itself does. Without signing in, the list of likes simply isn’t available.

Which platforms are supported?

Likes from Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Shuffler itself runs on iPhone (TestFlight beta), on Android and as a Chrome extension.

How do I report a problem?

In the app: Settings → Report a problem. Or write to danivichkin@gmail.com. More on the Support page.
